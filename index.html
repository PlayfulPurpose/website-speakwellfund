The problem

Exposure doesn't pay the rent, the mortgage or the groceries.

Far too often, speakers from underrepresented communities are asked to share their expertise for free, or for “exposure”, while everyone else in the room is being paid to be there. That adds up to a lot of invisible labour, and it means the voices we most need to hear are the ones who can least afford to keep showing up.

What we believe

A voice worth hearing is a voice worth backing.

We think recognising someone's expertise should mean backing it in ways that actually help, whether that's a fair fee, real support or the kind of visibility that leads to the next paid gig. Applause is lovely, but it doesn't cover the hours of preparation that go into a great talk.

Day one

We're right at the beginning, and that's the exciting part.

There's no fund yet and no fixed plan. Right now we're bringing together the people who care about this so we can work out, together, how to build a perpetual fund that keeps paying speakers year after year. If you've got ideas, experience or just a strong opinion about it, we'd love to hear from you.
