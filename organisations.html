For organisations

Great events are built on great speakers.

If you book speakers for conferences, panels, podcasts, workshops or team days, you have a real chance to make sure the people on your stage feel properly valued.

Why it matters

Unpaid speaking narrows who gets heard.

When speaking is unpaid, the people who can say yes are usually the ones whose employer covers their time or who don't need the income. That narrows the voices on your stage, and your audience misses out on the perspectives they came to hear.

What doing it well looks like

A fee matters, and so do the little things.

Paying a fee is the obvious one and it matters a lot, but valuing a speaker can also mean:

  • Covering travel, accommodation and childcare
  • Being upfront about budget in your very first email
  • Paying for preparation time on workshops and custom content
  • Promoting their work, book or business properly, with links and credit
  • Sharing the recording so they can use it to land their next booking

We're developing practical tools and templates to make all of this easier, and we'd love organisers to help us build them.
