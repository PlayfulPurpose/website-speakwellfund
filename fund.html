The Fund

A fund that keeps paying speakers, year after year.

The perpetual fund is the big idea at the heart of Speak Well Fund, and it's still taking shape. Here's roughly what we're imagining.

Why perpetual

Built to last, so the money keeps working.

A one-off pot of money gets spent and then it's gone. A perpetual fund is set up so the money keeps working and keeps paying speakers year after year, long after the first contributions come in. That's the idea, anyway. How we actually make it work is the part we haven't figured out yet.

What it could pay for

Some of what the fund could cover

  • Fair fees for speakers
  • Paid time to prepare a great talk or workshop
  • Travel and accommodation
  • Childcare and access needs, so more people can say yes
  • Coaching and support to help speakers grow

These are early ideas, and you can help decide what makes the list.

The big question

How might we build a fund that pays speakers fairly, year after year, and never runs dry?

We don't have the answer yet, and that's on purpose. We'd love to work it out together with people who know finance, philanthropy, grants or events, or who simply have a good idea.

Help us answer it

Speak Well Fund is just getting started, and we'd love you to help shape it.
