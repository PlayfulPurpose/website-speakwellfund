For speakers

You bring the expertise, so you deserve to be paid for it.

If you've ever been asked to give a talk, sit on a panel or run a workshop “for the exposure”, this is your community.

Sound familiar?

You were invited to give a keynote, and the budget was a lanyard and a sandwich.

You spent two weeks preparing a talk, and the thank-you was a tag on LinkedIn.

You were the only person on the panel who wasn't being paid by their employer to be there.

You said yes for free because you worried that saying no meant you'd never be asked again.

You're definitely not the only one, and it's a big part of why Speak Well Fund exists.

What we're building with you

Founding speakers get to shape this.

We want to build a community of speakers who back each other, and a perpetual fund that makes sure speakers from underrepresented communities are paid properly. Because we're at the start, the first speakers who join will help decide what this becomes, from how the fund works to what support speakers actually need.

How you can help right now

  1. Join the founding speaker circle and help shape this from day one.
  2. Tell us about the times you've been asked to speak for free, so we can show how common it really is.
  3. Pass this on to other speakers who'd want to be part of it.
