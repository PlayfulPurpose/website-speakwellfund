About

Why Speak Well Fund exists.

It started with a simple frustration: too many brilliant people from underrepresented communities are asked to share their expertise for free.

We want to change that by bringing together people who care about it and building a perpetual fund that keeps speakers paid and supported, year after year.

The people behind it

Started by Julie Trell

I started Speak Well Fund because I believe the people who share their expertise on stage deserve to be valued, and far too often they're asked to do it for free. I know because I've been one of them, and I've struggled to ask for the money myself. I've been thinking about this for years, but I found it hard to put into words well enough to get it out into the world, so it sat among a pile of half-baked ideas.

I bought the URL nearly two years ago, so it's well and truly time to activate it, and now with vibe coding I've been able to pull it all together and finally get it out there. I know I'm not alone in thinking this, and I'm hoping the right people show up so we can build the rest of it together, as humans. I'm looking for collaborators and accountability partners, so if that sounds like you, I'd love to hear from you.

Build this with me

Where we're headed

The plan is still being written, and you can help write it.

Over time, we'd love Speak Well Fund to include a curated speaker directory so organisers can find brilliant people, practical tools for organisations, and a perpetual fund that grows through memberships, partnerships and contributions. How we get there is still being worked out, and that's where you come in.
